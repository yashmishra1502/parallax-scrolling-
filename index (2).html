Day 1
Day 1, 5:40 am

four days up.

Field notes from a climb, cut out of paper.

Day 2

The pines start.

We left camp at six. By nine the cloud was below us, and the only sound was boots on needles.

Day 3

Long light.

The sun sat on the ridge for almost an hour. Nobody said anything, which felt about right.

Day 4

Cold, clear, quiet.

We reached the top in the dark and waited. Then the sky did the rest.
